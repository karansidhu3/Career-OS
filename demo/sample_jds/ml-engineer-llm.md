# ML Engineer — LLM Evaluation & Quality — Boreal AI

## About the role

Boreal builds language model APIs used by enterprise customers in legal, finance, and compliance. Reliability and factual accuracy matter more to our customers than raw benchmark scores. We need an ML engineer to own how we measure model quality — evaluation methodology, benchmark design, and the tooling that tells us whether a model regression is real or noise.

## What you'll do

- Design and run evaluation suites for instruction-following, factuality, refusal calibration, and constraint adherence
- Build tooling to measure whether models follow explicit formatting and topic constraints across paraphrase variants
- Develop datasets and annotation pipelines for legal and financial domain evaluation
- Analyze model failure modes — when and why do models fail to follow constraints, and what patterns predict failure
- Partner with the training team to translate evaluation findings into fine-tuning signals
- Own the evaluation infrastructure: pipelines, dashboards, result storage, reproducibility

## What we're looking for

- Strong machine learning fundamentals — you can read a paper on evaluation methodology and implement it
- Experience evaluating LLMs beyond standard benchmarks (MMLU, HellaSwag): you've thought about what you actually want to measure and designed tasks to measure it
- Proficiency in Python, PyTorch, and the Hugging Face ecosystem
- NLP background: you understand tokenization, attention, and fine-tuning well enough to reason about why a model behaves a certain way
- Experience with annotation pipelines and inter-annotator agreement — you've dealt with ambiguous labels
- Comfort with statistical analysis of evaluation results (significance testing, confidence intervals, correlation between metrics)

## Bonus

- You've built multi-dimensional evaluation frameworks that go beyond accuracy (factuality, calibration, consistency)
- You've worked with legal or financial text — you understand why domain shift is a problem
- You've contributed to research that used evaluation as the primary methodology

## Stack

Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React (for internal dashboards)

## Location

Remote (Canada preferred)
