# Software Engineer — Product (Full-Stack) — Slate

## About the role

Slate is a collaborative workspace for design and engineering teams. Think Figma meets Notion: real-time multiplayer, structured content, and a canvas that lets teams work the way they actually think. We're a small team (12 engineers) and every person owns a meaningful surface area.

We're looking for a full-stack engineer who cares about both the technical quality and the felt experience of the product — someone who notices when a transition feels off and knows how to fix it.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain real-time collaborative features: conflict resolution, presence, cursor sync, offline mode
- Own frontend performance — you know what makes a canvas feel fast versus sluggish and you can fix it
- Design and implement backend services in TypeScript/Node.js that support multiplayer state synchronization
- Contribute to the design of our CRDT-based sync protocol — we have opinions about this and want engineers who do too
- Work closely with design on interaction details; you should be comfortable pushing back when something isn't feasible and suggesting alternatives
- Participate in architecture decisions — we don't have a separate platform team, everyone shares the infrastructure work

## What we're looking for

- Strong TypeScript and React — you've built complex, stateful UIs and you understand the rendering model well enough to debug performance problems
- Real-time systems experience: WebSockets, CRDTs, or operational transformation — you've dealt with the concurrency problems that come with multiplayer
- Node.js backend: you can build an API, reason about async concurrency, and not leak connections
- Product sense — you use the products you build and you notice things that are slightly wrong
- Comfort with offline-first design: you understand IndexedDB, service workers, and the edge cases of sync-on-reconnect

## Bonus

- You've worked with Yjs, Automerge, or similar CRDT libraries
- You've built presence systems (cursor sync, live avatars, "who's editing this" indicators)
- Experience with canvas rendering, WebGL, or SVG at scale
- You've thought about what "real-time" means when the network is unreliable

## Stack

TypeScript, React, Node.js, WebSockets, PostgreSQL, Redis

## Location

Remote (North America)
