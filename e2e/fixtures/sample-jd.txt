Senior Software Engineer — Backend Platform

About the role:
We are building the infrastructure layer that powers our product analytics platform.
You will design, build, and operate services that handle high-throughput event ingestion,
real-time aggregation pipelines, and the data APIs consumed by our frontend.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement async Python services using FastAPI and asyncpg
- Own the PostgreSQL schema and query performance for our core data models
- Build and maintain Redis-backed caching and rate limiting layers
- Collaborate with the ML team to integrate model inference into the request path
- Write clear technical documentation and participate in architecture reviews

Requirements:
- 2+ years of professional experience with Python
- Strong familiarity with FastAPI or similar async frameworks
- Experience with PostgreSQL, query optimization, and schema design
- Understanding of distributed systems concepts (queues, caching, eventual consistency)
- Comfortable working with Docker and container-based deployments

Nice to have:
- Experience with vector databases (Qdrant, Pinecone, Weaviate)
- Familiarity with LLM APIs and prompt engineering
- Prior experience at a startup or small engineering team

We are a remote-first team with optional in-person hubs in Vancouver and Toronto.
Compensation: $110,000 — $145,000 CAD depending on experience.
