# AI Engineer — FGF Brands

FGF Brands is hiring an early-career AI engineer who already builds, prototypes, experiments, and ships. The engineer will work with supply-chain and operations leaders to turn business friction into production software.

Responsibilities include building agentic AI solutions, producing proofs of concept in days, applying LLMs to real data through RAG, fine-tuning, and agent frameworks, building internal tools and dashboards, evaluating emerging AI tools, and explaining systems through practical demos.

The role requires real AI projects, strong Python, APIs, version control, basic system design, hands-on work with LLMs, and an ability to explain technical ideas to non-technical partners.
